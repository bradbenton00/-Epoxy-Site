Common Questions

Epoxy Flooring FAQs — Radcliff, KY

How much does epoxy flooring cost in Radcliff, KY?

For a typical 2-car garage in the Radcliff area (400-500 sq ft), professional epoxy flooring runs roughly $4-$8 per square foot installed, or about $1,800-$3,500 total depending on the system and the condition of your concrete. We provide free written quotes — no pressure, no deposit required.

Are most Radcliff garage floors single-day jobs?

Yes. A typical 2-car garage in Radcliff runs 400–500 sq ft and is completed in one day — diamond grind, base coat, flake broadcast, and topcoat. Detached workshops and larger auto bays with more square footage may take two days. We'll give you an exact timeline in your free written estimate.

How long does epoxy flooring last in Kentucky's climate?

With proper diamond-ground prep and commercial-grade materials, epoxy and polyaspartic systems last 15-20+ years in Kentucky conditions. We work year-round and use cold-weather formulations for winter installs.

How long until I can park on my new garage floor?

With a polyaspartic system you can walk on the floor in 4-6 hours and park vehicles within 24 hours. Standard epoxy needs 24 hours for foot traffic and 3-5 days before parking heavy vehicles.
